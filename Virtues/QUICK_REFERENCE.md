3. **Diligence**: Commit to improvement

1. **Sacred Matrimony**: Reaffirm commitment
2. **Honesty**: Have difficult conversations
3. **Diligence**: Practice daily connection
4. **Grace**: Forgive quickly
5. **Chastity**: Maintain fidelity

2. **Fortitude**: Stand firm in choice
